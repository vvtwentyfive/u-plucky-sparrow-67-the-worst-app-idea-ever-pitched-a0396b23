## Entry

> LawnWatch livestreams your lawn to strangers who rate how fast your grass grows, with leaderboards, daily streaks, and a $4.99/month Pro tier that unlocks a second camera angle.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes a single sentence — one deadpan pitch for the worst, most useless app imaginable — to `/output/entry.txt`. The entry is fully deterministic: no network, no randomness, no input. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
